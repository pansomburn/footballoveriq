# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OverIQ is for live football bettors and match analysts who need to scan active matches quickly, understand pressure changes, and decide whether a match deserves attention before a key minute passes. Users are often watching multiple fixtures at once and need clear status, fast comparison, and trustworthy alerts more than decorative presentation.

## Product Purpose

OverIQ turns LSports live football data into a realtime decision dashboard. It calculates AI scores, detects live signal events, persists match snapshots, and notifies users when bookmarked matches hit important moments such as goals, pressure spikes, or score jumps. Success means users can identify the right match, understand why it matters, and act without missing the live window.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, trusted, focused. The product should feel like a serious analysis tool for high-tempo decisions: calm enough to trust, dense enough to scan, and clear enough to use under time pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards with oversized marketing cards, vague AI copy, decorative gradients, and metrics that look impressive but do not help a live decision. Avoid sports-betting clutter that shouts with too many colors, blinking states, or odds-heavy noise before the user understands the signal.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next important match obvious.
2. Explain every signal with enough evidence to earn trust.
3. Optimize for scanning before reading.
4. Separate realtime urgency from normal information.
5. Keep actions close to the match context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Do not rely on color alone for signal states; pair color with labels, icons, or shape. Motion should be short and state-driven, with reduced-motion support. Critical realtime states must remain readable on mobile and in bright ambient light.
